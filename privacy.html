Privacy policy

How we handle your personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Worldbrand Classic Signs (Reg. No. 2021/117042/07), 25 Sedgemoor Pl, Woodlands, Durban, 4004, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. Contact: info@worldbrandsigns.co.za.

What we collect

When you use our quote or contact form, you choose to send us your name, phone number, email address (optional) and details about your signage project. The form opens WhatsApp with your message — this website does not store your information on any server.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To plan, deliver and install your signage.
  • To keep records required for invoicing and tax.

We do not sell your information, and we don't send marketing without your permission.

Third parties

Messages sent through WhatsApp are handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy. Our contact page shows a Google Maps map, which is subject to Google's privacy policy. This website uses no tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email us at the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Keeping it safe

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above and take reasonable steps to protect it.
