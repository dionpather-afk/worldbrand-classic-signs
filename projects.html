Pizza Hut

We branded five Pizza Hut outlets in KwaZulu-Natal — Malvern, Bluff, Glenwood, Amanzimtoti and Scottburgh.

Vibrant, appetising visuals that draw customers in. From strategic placement to eye-catching design, the signage enhances the Pizza Hut experience and builds brand recognition on every street.

Ba-Ga Mothibi Community Health Centre

For the North West Department of Health, we branded the Ba-Ga Mothibi Community Health Centre in Sekhing Village, North West Province.

Clear wayfinding signs improve the patient experience and informative displays promote health awareness — creating a welcoming, efficient environment that builds trust within the community.
